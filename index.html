About Me

Honestly speaking, my journey as an electronics and computer engineer started long before I even knew what engineering was. From connecting different electronic equipment like televisions to decoders during my primary school era, to assembling a desktop computer during my secondary school years, I have always been interested in understanding how things work.
When my Malawi School Certificate of Education (MSCE) results were out, I made sure to apply for electronics and computer engineering at MUBAS and by God's grace I was taken in. Since then I have been learning both hardware, software and everything in between. It has been an exciting journey up to now and I am looking forward to internships and work opportunities that may arise in the near future.
